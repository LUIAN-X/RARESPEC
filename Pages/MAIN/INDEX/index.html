KADUPUL FLOWER

Epiphyllum oxypetalum

The Kadupul flower is a rare and beautiful night-blooming flower native to Sri Lanka and other tropical regions. It usually blooms at night and withers before morning, making it very difficult to see in full bloom. Its delicate white petals and pleasant fragrance make it a symbol of beauty, purity, and mystery.

GIANT HORNBILL

Buceros bicornis

Giant Hornbill, is one of the world's largest hornbill species, recognized by its massive yellow casque and impressive wingspan. Found in the forests of southern Nepal, it plays a vital role in seed dispersal, helping maintain healthy forest ecosystems, but is threatened by habitat loss and hunting

SNOW LEOPARD

Panthera uncia

The snow leopard (Panthera uncia) is a rare and endangered big cat found in the high Himalayan mountains of Nepal. It is threatened by habitat loss, poaching, and climate change. In Nepal, it is classified as Vulnerable, with an estimated population of around 300–400 individuals, making conservation efforts very important.

Chocolate Cosmos

Cosmos atrosanguineus

The Chocolate Cosmos (Cosmos atrosanguineus) is a beautiful, rare flower known for its deep chocolate-brown to maroon petals. It produces a sweet fragrance that resembles the smell of chocolate, especially during warm weather. This unique flower is native to Mexico and is popular for its attractive color and delightful scent.
