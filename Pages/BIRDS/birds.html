BIRDS WORLD

Birds are warm-blooded animals with feathers, wings, and beaks, and most can fly using strong muscles and lightweight bones. They are found all over the world in different environments like forests, deserts, and oceans. Birds vary in size, shape, color, and diet, feeding on seeds, insects, fish, or small animals. They reproduce by laying eggs and usually build nests to care for their young. Many birds are also known for their songs and communication. Birds help maintain ecological balance by controlling insects and spreading seeds. Some birds act as pollinators and scavengers, cleaning the environment. Overall, birds are essential for biodiversity and a healthy ecosystem.

Name: Mynah

Scientific Name: Acridotheres tristis

Scientific Classification

Kingdom: Animalia

Phylum: Chordata

Class: Aves

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Sturnidae

Genus: Acridotheres

Description: Mynah is a medium-sized bird known for its striking black or brown body, bright yellow eye patches, and strong, curved beak. It is famous for its ability to mimic human speech and other sounds very accurately. Mynahs are highly social birds, often seen in pairs or small groups, and are commonly found in urban areas, forests, and open fields. They play an important ecological role by feeding on insects, fruits, and small invertebrates, helping in natural pest control.

Species Of Mynah:

1) Hill Mynah

Scientific Name: Gracula religiosa

Description 👉: The Hill Mynah is a beautiful black-colored bird. It has bright yellow-orange wattles near its head.Its scientific name is Gracula religiosa. The bird is famous for copying human speech and sounds.It is commonly found in forests and hilly regions. The Hill Mynah mainly eats fruits, insects, and nectar. It is an intelligent and social bird often seen in pairs or groups. It is mostly found in countries like India, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand, and Indonesia. In Nepal, Hill Mynahs are mainly found in the Terai and lower hilly forest areas such as Chitwan, Bardia, Koshi Tappu, and Shivapuri.

2) Bank Mynah

Scientific Name: Acridotheres ginginianus

Description 👉: The Bank Myna (Acridotheres ginginianus) is a small grey bird with a black head, orange-red bare skin around the eyes, and a distinctive loud call. It is commonly seen in open lands, cities, and near rivers where it often lives in groups. In Nepal, it is mostly found in the Terai region, especially in areas like Biratnagar, Janakpur, and Nepalgunj. It prefers lowland urban and agricultural habitats. Globally, it is mainly distributed across the Indian subcontinent, including India, Pakistan, and Bangladesh. It thrives in warm climates and is closely associated with human settlements.

Name: House Sparrow

Scientific Name: Passer domesticus

Scientific Classification

Kingdom: Animalia

Phylum: Chordata

Class: Aves

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Passeridae

Genus: Passer

Description: The House Sparrow is a small brown and grey bird commonly found near houses, farms, and cities. It is social in nature and usually lives in groups close to human settlements. House sparrows help the ecosystem by eating insects and pests, which protects crops and gardens. They also help in seed dispersal and serve as food for larger birds and animals, maintaining the natural food chain.

Species Of House Sparrow:

1) Tree Sparrow

Scientific Name: Passer montanus

Description 👉: The Tree Sparrow (Passer montanus) is a small brown bird with a chestnut-colored head, white cheeks, and a black spot on each cheek. It usually lives in forests, farmlands, villages, and open countryside areas. In Nepal, tree sparrows are commonly found in hilly and mountain regions, including areas around Kathmandu and Pokhara. Around the world, they are widely distributed across Europe and Asia, especially in countries like China, Japan, and Russia.

2) Spanish Sparrow

Scientific Name: Passer hispaniolensis

Description 👉: The Spanish Sparrow (Passer hispaniolensis) is a small brown sparrow with black streaks and a strong, short beak. It usually lives in grasslands, farmlands, and open woodland areas, often staying in large flocks. In Nepal, the Spanish Sparrow is very rare and is occasionally seen in lowland Terai regions during migration. Around the world, it is mainly found in Spain, Turkey, Egypt, and parts of the Mediterranean and Middle East. It feeds mostly on seeds, grains, and small insects.

Name: Eagle

Scientific Classification

Kingdom: Animalia

Phylum: Chordata

Class: Aves

Order: Accipitriformes

Family: Accipitridae

Genus: Aquila

Description: Eagle are strong birds of prey with sharp eyesight, powerful claws, and hooked beaks. They are found in mountains, forests, and grasslands around the world. In Nepal, eagles are mostly found in the Himalayan region and forest areas like Chitwan National Park. They help the ecosystem by controlling small animal populations and cleaning dead animals from nature.

Species of Eagle:

1) Golden Eagle

Scientific Name: Aquila chrysaetos

Description 👉: The Golden Eagle (Aquila chrysaetos) is a large and powerful bird of prey known for its golden-brown feathers and sharp eyesight. It is commonly found in mountains, cliffs, grasslands, and open forests around the world. In Nepal, it is mostly found in the Himalayan and hilly regions.Eagles help the ecosystem by controlling populations of small mammals and birds, keeping nature balanced. They also remove weak or dead animals, helping keep the environment clean.

2) Mountain Hawk-Eagle

Scientific Name: Nisaetus nipalensis

Description 👉: The Mountain Hawk-Eagle (Nisaetus nipalensis) is a powerful bird of prey with sharp eyesight, strong claws, and a crest on its head. It is commonly found in forests and mountainous regions of Asia. In Nepal, it is mostly found in forest areas of Langtang National Park, Annapurna Conservation Area, and Shivapuri Nagarjun National Park.This eagle helps the ecosystem by controlling populations of small mammals, reptiles, and birds, which keeps the forest food chain balanced. Its presence also shows a healthy forest environment.

Name: Cheer Pheasant

Scientific Name: Catreus wallichii

Scientific Classification

Kingdom: Animalia

Phylum: Chordata

Class: Aves

Order: Galliformes

Family: Phasianidae

Genus: Catreus

Description: The Cheer Pheasant is a rare Himalayan bird with a long tail, brown-grey body, and a loud, far-carrying call. It lives in steep grassy slopes and scrub forests of high hills. In Nepal, it is mostly found in western regions such as Dhorpatan, Rolpa, Rukum, and Myagdi. Its Nepali name is “Cheer” (चीर फिजेन्ट) or sometimes locally called “Chir Pheasant / Chir”. It helps the ecosystem by feeding on seeds, plants, insects, and small invertebrates, which supports seed dispersal and insect control in its habitat.

Subspecies of Cheer Pheasant: The Cheer Pheasant has no officially recognized subspecies. It is the only species in the genus Catreus.

Name: White-rumped Vulture

Scientific Classification

Kingdom: Animalia

Phylum: Chordata

Class: Aves

Order: Accipitriformes

Family: Accipitridae

Genus: Gyps

Description: The White-rumped Vulture (Gyps bengalensis) is a large scavenger bird with a white lower back (rump), pale body, and dark wings. This vulture helps the ecosystem by eating dead animals, which prevents the spread of diseases and keeps the environment clean and balanced.

Subspecies of White-rumped Vulture: The White-rumped Vulture is a single species of vulture found in South Asia. The White-rumped Vulture has no officially recognized subspecies.

Name: Great Hornbill

Scientific Name:

Scientific Classification

Kingdom: Animalia

Phylum: Chordata

class: Aves

Order: Bucerotiformes

Family: Bucerotidae

Genus:: Buceros

Species: Buceros bicornis

Description: The Great Hornbill (Buceros bicornis) is a large, colorful bird known for its huge yellow casque on top of its beak and strong curved bill. It is mainly found in the dense tropical and subtropical forests of South and Southeast Asia. In Nepal, it is mostly found in the eastern and central lowland forests, especially in protected areas like Chitwan National Park and Bardia region. This bird plays an important role in the ecosystem by spreading seeds of fruits it eats, helping forest regeneration. It also helps maintain biodiversity by supporting healthy forest growth. The Great Hornbill prefers old, tall trees for nesting and is considered an important indicator of healthy forest ecosystems.

Some Species of Great Hornbill

1) Rhinoceros Hornbill

It mainly lives in tropical rainforests and feeds on fruits, insects, and small animals. This bird helps the ecosystem by spreading seeds and supporting forest growth. Rhinoceros Hornbills are mostly found in countries like Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, and Brunei. In Nepal, this bird is not commonly found and there are very few official records of it in the wild.

Scientific Name: Anthracoceros albirostris)
