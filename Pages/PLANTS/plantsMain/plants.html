Plants are living organisms that belong to the kingdom Plantae kingdom. They are essential for life on Earth because they produce oxygen and food through photosynthesis. Photosynthesis is a process that uses sunlight,water and carbon dioxide.
Plants come in many forms, including trees, shrubs, grasses, and flowers. They usually have roots to absorb water and nutrients, stems to support the plant, and leaves where photosynthesis happens. Plants are the base of most food chains and play a vital role in maintaining ecological balance.
